Two ways to trade

Every country has one live session at a set time. Practice sessions are yours to start.

Live session

Trade alongside other people at a fixed time. This is the session that counts.

  • One session per country, at a set time
  • You trade against other people and a crowd of automated participants
  • Profit, loss and tax statements are recorded on your account

Practice session

Start one whenever you like and try things out. Nothing here goes on your record.

  • You choose the length, from 1 to 2 hours
  • You trade against automated participants only
  • Pick the market conditions, or let it surprise you

How it works

  1. 1

    Open your accounts

    You start with a trading balance and a bank balance, like a real account. Move money between them whenever you need to.

  2. 2

    Pick your market

    Choose India, the US or the UK. Each has its own invented companies and its own session time.

  3. 3

    Join a session

    Join the live session when it opens, or start a practice session. Live results are kept on your record.

Not another paper-trading app

Your orders move the price

On most paper-trading apps your order never touches the market. Here it meets a live order book, so big orders slip and thin markets push back.

The market doesn't wait

Live sessions open and close on a clock, for everyone at once. You can't pause one or rewind it.

Running out costs time

If your balance runs low it refills slowly, and each wipe-out makes the wait longer. Losses feel like losses, with no real money involved.

Nothing here is real

Companies, tickers and exchanges are invented, so there are no real prices to copy and no real money to lose.

Questions

Is this real money?

No. Balances, companies and exchanges are all synthetic. You can't deposit, withdraw or buy anything real.

Who am I trading against?

In live sessions, other people and a crowd of automated participants. In practice sessions, automated participants only.

What happens if I run out of money?

Your trading balance refills slowly, and each time you run out the wait gets longer. You can also move money across from your bank balance.

When can I get in?

Early access is by invite. Request yours with your email and we'll tell you when it's ready.
